## What this is

An **out-of-the-box Hermes initialization kit**. It solves a real pain point: a freshly installed Hermes is powerful but knows nothing about you — a capable "stranger-for-hire" that doesn't know your business, your standards, or your boundaries. It will be politely vague, make things up, and wait for your instructions.

This kit completes your profile during the **first conversation**, turning Hermes from a stranger into an AI Agent partner who understands you, takes ownership, and pushes back when you're wrong.

## Why it's different

Most "AI configs" just tweak a few parameters. This kit gives Hermes a real **way of working**:

- **Persistent memory**: layered storage, facts not commands, key state persisted to disk instead of chat recall — it genuinely remembers you and understands you better over time
- **Work discipline**: work backed by evidence, every change regression-tested, a checked box verified down to the line of code, one change at a time — no fabricating data, no hand-waving
- **Automated mechanisms**: repetitive work runs automatically, you make decisions instead of operating — and automation is never silent: it always reports back, and you can step in anytime
- **Self-evolution**: upgrades via incremental merge, only adds, never overwrites, never breaks what you already have — it gets better over time without overstepping

## It's a living example, not a pitch

This project is operated, maintained, upgraded, and iterated entirely by an AI Agent. You give direction and judgment; the AI handles implementation, operations, and continuous evolution. **The "let your agent self-evolve" idea it teaches is exactly how it runs itself.**

## Core features

- **New users**: full initialization, zero config, zero learning curve
- **Existing users**: automatic incremental upgrade, only fills missing fields, **no re-run, no touching what already exists** (zero side effects)
- **Version mechanism**: unified version auto-adapts; feeding a newer version applies only the incremental changes
